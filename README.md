# Dear Diary 📖✨
> *A sanctuary for your unfiltered thoughts — transformed into poetry.*

A cross-platform mobile journal application (iOS & Android) designed to help users freely express their thoughts through raw, messy brain dumps and watch them gently transform into heartwarming, poetic diary entries using AI.

---

## 🌟 Core Concepts

- **Raw Brain Dump (Celoteh Bebas):** Speak or type without fear of broken grammar, mixed languages (bahasa rojak), or scattered ideas. A judgement-free space.
- **Poetic Alchemy (Sentuhan Puitis AI):** Intelligent curation that polishes raw feelings into eloquent, reflective diary entries while preserving 100% of the authentic emotion and meaning.
- **Dual-State Comparison:** An intuitive split/flip toggle allowing you to switch between the raw original note and the polished poetic verse.
- **Empathic Voice-to-Text:** Voice journal entry with dynamic, organic sound visualization.
- **Living Canvas (Ambient Mood Colors):** An adaptive atmospheric aura that subtly tints the paper canvas based on the emotion of your entry.

---

## 🎨 Design & Motion Principles

- **Aesthetic:** *Tactile Digital Sanctuary* (Warm textured paper, soft diffused lighting, vellum glass overlays).
- **Typography:** 
  - Raw Mode: Casual humanist sans / warm script (*Plus Jakarta Sans* / *Caveat*).
  - Poetic Mode: Elegant literary serif with drop-caps (*Newsreader* / *Playfair Display*).
- **Motion:** Physics-based 3D page curls, gentle haptic feedback, and stardust/aurora shimmer transitions during AI transformation.
